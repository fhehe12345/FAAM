### How does effective communication reduce project risks?
Effective communication aligns development, data preparation, and testing activities. By sharing progress and feedback consistently, potential problems such as usability or performance issues can be resolved early.

### Which stakeholder is most critical to your project’s success and why?
End users are the most critical stakeholders because their needs and feedback directly influence the usability, clarity, and effectiveness of the medical chatbot.
