### How does effective communication reduce project risks?
Good communication supports risk management by keeping everyone informed about changes, challenges, and quality checks. This coordination helps the team respond efficiently to emerging issues.

### Which stakeholder is most critical to your project’s success and why?
The project team is the most critical stakeholder since their collaboration determines data quality, model performance, system safety, and successful project delivery.
