## Performance Analysis

The tasks showing the largest deviation from the project plan are **System Design (2.2)**, **Module B Development (3.2)**, and **Unit Testing (4.1)**.

System Design is behind schedule due to extended discussions on system architecture and the need for additional quality and safety reviews for the medical chatbot. Module B Development is delayed because it depends on the completion of Module A, causing a cascading schedule impact. Unit Testing progress is lower than planned as development tasks have not reached sufficient maturity for full testing.

These issues are mainly related to **resource constraints**, **schedule dependencies**, and **risk management considerations** identified earlier, particularly risks associated with model accuracy, system safety, and limited development resources. No major scope changes were identified, but additional review cycles have contributed to the delays.
