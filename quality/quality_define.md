# **AI Project – Quality Definition**

## **1. What must your AI system do to be considered successful?**

An AI system is considered successful when it:

- Accurately understands and processes user input using NLP.  
- Provides reliable, medically consistent guidance based on symptoms.  
- Responds quickly and clearly with easy-to-understand explanations.  
- Helps users decide whether self-care is enough or medical attention is needed.  
- Maintains safe, non-diagnostic advice aligned with medical best practices.  
- Achieves **≥ 85% accuracy** in its responses during evaluation.  
- Ensures a smooth, user-friendly experience on the chosen platform(s).  

---

## **2. What will you measure to confirm the output is of acceptable quality?**

You will measure quality using a combination of technical, performance, and user satisfaction metrics:

### **Technical Accuracy**
- **Precision, Recall, and F1-Score** – to measure how correctly symptoms and intents are interpreted.  
- **Knowledge accuracy** – correctness of medical suggestions based on verified sources.  

### **Performance**
- **Average response time** – target under 2 seconds for smooth interaction.  
- **Error rate** – frequency of incorrect, irrelevant, or unsafe responses.  

### **User Experience**
- **User satisfaction score** – collected through surveys or feedback.  
- **Task completion rate** – ability of users to get helpful guidance without escalation.  
- **User retention** – how often users return to use the chatbot.  

---

## **3. What tools or processes will help you ensure quality?**

### **Tools**
- **MS Project** – for scheduling, monitoring milestones, and managing progress.  
- **GitHub** – for code version control, issue tracking, and collaborative development.  
- **Python + NLP/ML libraries** (spaCy, NLTK, TensorFlow, PyTorch) – for model training and evaluation.  
- **Miro/Lucidchart** – for workflow, architecture, and data flow diagrams.  
- **User testing tools** – to collect feedback and measure usability.  

### **Processes**
- **Data cleaning and preprocessing** – ensures high-quality training data.  
- **Model training + iterative evaluation** – continuously improving accuracy using metrics.  
- **Knowledge base validation** – ensuring medical content is consistent and up-to-date.  
- **Continuous Integration/Testing (CI/CD)** – automated testing to detect issues early.  
- **User Acceptance Testing (UAT)** – real users evaluate accuracy and clarity.  
- **Error logging and refinement loops** – systematic monitoring and model updates.
