# Quality Definition (Week 06)

- **Accuracy target:**  
  ≥ 85% response accuracy measured using Precision, Recall, and F1-Score.

- **Performance expectations:**  
  Average response time under 2 seconds, low error rate, and stable operation with minimal downtime.

- **User interface/usability expectations:**  
  Simple, intuitive, and user-friendly interface; clear medical explanations; smooth interaction flow on both web and mobile platforms.

- **Data quality requirements:**  
  Clean, verified, and medically reliable datasets; properly preprocessed text; consistent labeling; and a validated medical knowledge base derived from trusted health sources.
